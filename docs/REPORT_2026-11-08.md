## 2. AILU World: the "blank page" when moving up or down

**Cause.** In the generated `engine.ts`, a vertical drag tilts the camera (its "pitch") up to about 34° up or 43° down.
Looking up shows only the empty sky (black). Looking down shows only the floor (brown). Visitors read both as a blank page.
Confirmed in a browser at 390px before the fix.

**Fix.** `src/components/AiluWorld/levelView.ts`. `engine.ts` is generated and must not be edited, so this file sits in front of it
(like `phoneTurn.ts`). It passes every drag to the engine with the finger's height fixed at where the press started.
The engine sees only sideways movement, so the world turns left and right only.
- A quick up or down flick no longer counts as a tap, so it cannot open a place by accident.
- Room stops that are planned to look up or down (like "Up among the balloons") still work. Only the visitor's drag is level.
- Tests: `levelView.test.ts`. Checked in the browser: after big up/down drags the view stays level; sideways drag still turns.
- For the next world regeneration: the real fix is in the engine (see `CHANGES_FOR_GENERATOR.md`, Engine item 11).

## 3. AILU World: every step says what it is

Before, a room card showed only the stop title, a paragraph, "2 of 5" and "Keep going".
Visitors could not see the steps of the room or what came next.

Now (file `src/components/AiluWorld/worldSteps.ts`, styles in `world-brand.css`):
- A line with the room name and step: **WEDDING · STEP 2 OF 5**.
- A row of numbered chips with short names: **1 Entrance · 2 Aisle · 3 Mandap · 4 Dinner · 5 Sangeet**.
  The current one is yellow, the ones already seen are marked. Each chip can be tapped to jump there.
  On phones the row scrolls sideways inside the card. The page itself never scrolls sideways.
- The next button names the next stop: **Next: Aisle →**. On the last stop it says **Last stop**.
- Rooms covered: Events hall, Wedding, Birthday, Corporate, Stage night, Community, People, Culture, About AILU, Dreams.
- `worldSteps.test.ts` reads `engine.ts` and checks that every step name matches the engine's real stops, in order.
  If the world is regenerated and a stop changes, this test fails and says which one.
- Checked in the browser at 390px and 1440px.
