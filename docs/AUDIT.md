## Plan: two-worlds structure

Assumption: "two worlds" means **World 1 = the immersive 360° AILU world** (entry experience) and **World 2 = the classic site** (Navbar, footer, real forms, CRM-backed). Both share one brand, one content source and one set of forms.

1. **Decide the URLs (ask Rohith).** Recommended: `/` = world, all existing pages stay where they are, `/classic` is removed (or becomes a no-JS fallback). Replace the 308 `/world` redirect with a 307, or remove it.
2. **Route groups.** Move pages into `src/app/(world)/page.tsx` and `src/app/(site)/…` with their own `layout.tsx`. `(site)/layout.tsx` renders Navbar, Footer, FloatingWhatsApp and `<main>`. `(world)/layout.tsx` renders a lightweight world shell. This replaces the `pathname === "/"` check in `SiteChrome`, and the world CSS loads only in that layout.
3. **One content source.** Move the world copy out of `body.ts` into `src/content/site.ts`. Fix the contradictions (#3), remove the points preview and the in-world forms (#2). Every world "action" links to a real `(site)` route through one mapping table covering all public routes (#1).
4. **Brand-align the world.** Use `next/font` Poppins/Inter and the three brand colors, and scope or prefix keyframes and resets (#7, #8).
5. **Harden the engine.** Dispose WebGL, scope DOM queries to `ROOT`, stop setting the title, honour reduced motion and low-power devices with the existing `canUse3D()` (#9). Then remove `@ts-nocheck` / the eslint ignore one module at a time.
6. **Fix the transitions between worlds.** Decide where the Intro plays (#4), make "Back to the world" and "Classic site" links consistent, and keep the mobile navbar to one row.
7. **Clean up.** Delete `public/ailu-celebrations.html`, `src/app/world`, unused components and `firebase-admin`. Fix `.gitignore`.
8. **Verify.** Run lint, tsc, tests and build. Do a real-device walkthrough at 390px and on desktop (intro, world → each form → submit, WhatsApp button overlap). Add a smoke test for SiteChrome/layout routing. Then continue with `LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md`.
